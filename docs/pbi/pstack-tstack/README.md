## Decisions for Sven

- **L1. Two kinds of round.** Squad today: "At most two fix rounds" ([`plugins/squad/skills/squad/SKILL.md:108`](https://github.com/svennijhuis/agentPacks/blob/34e1dec3535baa1d77cb441ea8c98498dc9b3684/plugins/squad/skills/squad/SKILL.md#L108), [`commands/squad.md:35`](https://github.com/svennijhuis/agentPacks/blob/34e1dec3535baa1d77cb441ea8c98498dc9b3684/plugins/squad/commands/squad.md#L35)). sol-loop wants 3 review rounds (5 for risky code). The room runs Reviewer PASS/FAIL. Proposal: keep both and name them apart.
  - **Fix round**: inside one `/squad` run. Automatic, uncommitted, max 2. No change.
  - **Review round**: on a PR, Reviewer bot against Builder, through `/squad-review`. Budget 3, or 5 for security, auth, data loss or concurrency. Round 1 is the Reviewer's first PASS/FAIL. PASS = no blocker or should open. Out of budget = BLOCKED for Sven, never PASS.
  - Option B (one budget for both) means changing `SquadContractTests`, the orchestrator and the room rule. Not recommended.
- **L2. One set of severity words.** Main has `high | medium | low | tiny` ([`review-contract.md:5-14`](https://github.com/svennijhuis/agentPacks/blob/34e1dec3535baa1d77cb441ea8c98498dc9b3684/plugins/squad/skills/squad/references/review-contract.md#L5-L14)). sol-loop has `blocker | should | nit`. AP25 (planned) has `severe | important | nit`. Proposal: keep the 4 words in the files. `blocker` = high, `should` = medium, `nit` = low or tiny, as one alias table in `review-contract.md ## Severity`. AP25 uses the same alias and adds no third set.
- **L3.** D39 and D40 Source rows: Strategy adds them to `url-learnings.md`.
