# Advice block

Fill this on the gap row before the one grill question.

Skills for this block: `architect` (TD2+) and `principle-exhaust-the-design-space` (TD2+) for the options, `codebase-design` when a seam is in play, `why` (TD2+) for the source, `blast-radius` (TD2+) for the risk line.

```markdown
## Advice

Options:
1. <option>
2. <option>
3. <option, only when a real third exists>

Recommended: <one option>
Why: <reason>. Source: <file, spec, or research note>
Risk if accepted-risk: <what breaks, and who carries it>
```

Offer 2 to 3 options. `why` (TD2+) names the source. `blast-radius` (TD2+) writes the risk line.

Then call `grilling` and `domain-modeling`. Ask the gap's question once. Include the recommended option and why.

The human or the dependency owner chooses. Their answer sets `decided`. Their acceptance of the risk line sets `accepted-risk`. Advice alone never closes a gap.

Failure: the row is `decided` and the only text on it is this block.
